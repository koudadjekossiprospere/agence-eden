What you’ll plan with The Eden Plan

Eleven steps, in the right order.

  1. 01

    Your vision and priorities

    Before you start spending, identify together what truly matters for your wedding.

  2. 02

    Your budget

    Build a realistic budget, allocate your expenses and set aside a margin for the unexpected.

  3. 03

    Your timeline

    Know what to do several months before the wedding, then as the big day approaches.

  4. 04

    Your vendors

    Learn which questions to ask before signing, what to check in contracts and how to spot certain red flags.

  5. 05

    Your guests

    Organize your guest list, your RSVP tracking and your seating chart.

  6. 06

    Legal and logistical essentials

    Anticipate the administrative steps and practical details that absolutely must not be forgotten.

  7. 07

    Design and decor

    Turn your inspiration into a more cohesive vision while keeping your budget in mind.

  8. 08

    Your wedding-day timeline

    Build a realistic schedule for the big day and appoint a point person, so you don’t have to manage the vendors yourselves during your wedding.

  9. 09

    The final countdown

    Prepare for the last few weeks with a clear method.

  10. 10

    Mistakes to avoid

    Learn from real situations encountered in wedding planning to avoid certain costly or stressful mistakes.

  11. 11

    Bonus: emergency kit and Plan B

    Prepare for the unexpected, including rain, delays and the little hiccups of the big day.
